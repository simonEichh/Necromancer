2. WHAT TO CARRY OVER FROM MALAK

Malak works because his entire presentation describes one personality. His restrained gestures, hologram, armillary drone, orbital basic, supply-pod special, ship arrival, and Fleet Strike belong to the same character. His ship enlarges the shapes already present in his helmet, coat blades, and drone. His basic attack hints at a larger source of power which his finisher finally reveals.

Apply that principle to Veyr:

- One visual vocabulary: hood, skeletal hands, tattered mantle, blackened gothic rifle, blue-violet soul light, concentric arcane sights, floating focusing shards.
- One personality: a patient, silent executioner. He has already found the weak point. He expends almost no motion until the shot.
- One escalation: a few focusing rings for an ordinary shot, a much larger accelerator for the special, an impossible procession of sights for the gate finisher.

Build the animations slower than your first instinct. It is easier to shorten a readable performance than rescue an unreadably fast one. Use Malak's engineering patterns where they fit. Veyr should have his own arrival, effects, silhouette, and performance. Avoid introducing a ship, orbital bombardment, drone swarm, or military hologram into his kit merely because Malak already has them.
