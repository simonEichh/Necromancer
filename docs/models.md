- **Spell casts / barrages (`AttackStyle = "StaffCast"`)**: a unit model with this attribute (plus `CastColor` Color3 and `CastPart`, the part at the staff tip) is a staff caster. It walks to its formation slot, raises its staff out in front of it and **fires a continuous barrage while the server says `Barrage` is true** (it stops when the unit walks again or is removed); the stance, a turning magic circle at the staff tip and the orbs all follow that. The staff needs the `RStaff` joint (forearm -> invisible `StaffRoot` holding the staff parts). **Every orb is a real shot that damages the wall when it lands, without any per-shot networking**: `Army.startBarrage` sets the model attributes `Barrage`, `BarrageStart` (server time of shot 0, 0.4 s ahead), `BarrageRate` (shots/s, `def.shotsPerSecond` or 6), `ShotTime` (flight time, from distance / 30) and `AimPos`; shot k fires at `BarrageStart + k/rate` and lands `ShotTime` later. The server (`Army.settleBarrages`, once per plot per frame) applies the damage of landed shots in 0.1 s batches (the wall's HP bar shows the hits; barrage shots send no `WallHit`, the orbs draw their own impacts); per-shot damage = (damage / attackCD) / rate so DPS is unchanged. Clients (`Modules/SpellEffects`) derive the same schedule from the attributes with `workspace:GetServerTimeNow()`, fly each orb for exactly `ShotTime` (impact drawn 0.1 s later, about when the HP bar update arrives), draw at most 8 orbs/s per caster (every stride-th shot, bigger) while the damage still counts every shot, and are capped at 14 circles / 90 orbs, skipped beyond 220 studs. `UnitAnimator` (`castPose`, `isCasting`, `castAmount`) animates the stance. Done so far: `LichAcolyte` (violet). To give another staff unit the same attack: build its staff on a `StaffRoot` + `RStaff` joint like `build_lichacolyte.lua` and add the three attributes. Other units still deal their damage instantly per attack (Goblin arrows land on arrival).

**Model conventions (every tier)**
- Folder `ReplicatedStorage.GraveModels.<Key>` (client-visible; no publish step needed). The script replaces an existing model and its preview copy.
- Origin = floor centre: PrimaryPart `Base` (a round ground patch, a Cylinder rotated upright; set `base.PivotOffset = base.CFrame:Inverse()` or the model pivot ends up tipped over). FRONT faces -Z (the camera looks from -Z). Footprint about 5-8 studs, height 3-5 (the cinematics scale the model by 1.5).
- Child models: `Body` (stays), `Lid` (what bursts away), `Pit` (the revealed inside: dark floor plus a few thin `Glow` cracks; NEVER a neon-edged rectangle, the user rejected it as "too modern"; ragged and natural instead). Part `Core` (invisible, 0.4 cube) where the light column and the unit come out, set at about the pit floor height.
- Parts named `Glow` (Neon) are the soul-light cracks/seams: the opening tints them towards the rarity colour during the buildup and fully at the burst. Every tier needs some on Body and/or Lid so the buildup hint is visible.
- Attributes: `DebrisColor` (optional, colour of the burst debris), `OpenStyle` ("Mound" = earth mound vanishes under the flash and small pieces fly; "Lid" = the whole Lid flies off as ONE rigid piece, tumbling; planned: "Doors" for crypts/vaults, "Shatter" for Void Sanctum / crystal ones; add new styles in `GraveOpening.burst`), `GlowColor` (the tier's own glow), `Height`.
- Build helpers used so far: `part(parent, name, size, cf, color, material, shape)`, `disc` (upright cylinder), `edgeSlab` (a slab along an outline edge, for non-rectangular shapes like the coffin), `bracket` (iron L-plates with rivets on a corner). Keep tabs, `-- ====` section banners, all parts Anchored, CanCollide false, CastShadow false.
- Look: dark gothic, chunky, readable silhouette; each tier its own signature colour from its concept (Mossy moss green, Stone pale white light, Ancient amber/sandstone, Cursed violet + chains, Shadow blue-violet black basalt, Abyssal magma red-orange, Eldritch teal + eye + tentacles, Void purple floating obsidian, Eternal white marble + gold, Celestial icy blue-white crystal + stars). Neon in very pale colours blows out to white under the bloom: use saturated mid colours for glow.
