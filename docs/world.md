# The world (Studio place file)

Moved out of CLAUDE.md. Everything here lives only in the place file (built by the scripts in `tools/studio/`) or is created at runtime by the server.

## World (Studio, not in repo)

- **Coliseum layout** (built by `tools/studio/build_coliseum.lua`, safe to re-run; the server derives everything from the parts it places): a circular plaza (`Workspace.HubFloor`, radius 56, centred on the origin, floor top Y=-1.7) with the 8 plots around it, plot i pointing outward at compass angle (i-1)*45 degrees. Plots are claimed per player.
- **Wall models (Realm 1)**: walls 1-3 are the Cemetery wall (`tools/studio/build_wall_cemetery.lua` -> `ServerStorage.WallModels.Cemetery`, ~350 parts, place file only: a crescent fieldstone wall ~69 wide that envelops the 40-wide lane, iron fence, stone cross, lantern pillars, tombstones, dead trees, standing on its own earth bank 0.3 below the lane floor (closer z-fights) with collision for players (`Decor` group, units ignore it)). The gate (wall 4) is `CemeteryGate` (`tools/studio/build_wall_cemetery_gate.lua`, ~354 parts: a mausoleum with an angel, an iron double door chained shut, soul light inside, green-fire braziers, taller wings). `Walls.spawnWall` clones the model into the slab part, which stays as the invisible collider (no model = plain slab). Damage stages: the server sets the model attribute `Stage` from the HP (1 at 75 %, 2 at 50 %, 3 at 25 %, 4 = collapse on break, removed after 3 s); client `Modules/WallStages` animates the grouped pieces (`Segment` (attributes `Sink`, `NoCracks`), `Picket`, `Grave`, `Lantern`, `Cross`/`Angel`, `Door`, `Chains`, `Brazier` models) and waits until all `Parts` (attribute) have replicated before registering a wall. Plan: `Design.md` (realms, titans, gate model, lava, veil).
- `Workspace.Plots.Plot1..Plot8`: each has `Base` (size 40 across x 2 x 48 along the lane; its **LookVector is the lane direction, outward**), `WallOrigin` (no longer used: the front wall's distance is the constant `Walls.WALL_FWD`), `PlayerSpawn` (+8 along the lane), `GraveSpot1..10` (invisible markers: 2 columns at -10 / -18 along the lane x 5 rows at -14..14 across, spaced for the 6 x 5.7 tombstone). The Base's inner edge is 60 studs from the centre.
  - **The army advances down the lane to the gate.** A run is `Economy.WALLS_PER_RUN` = 4 walls; ONLY the wall being fought stands: when a wall breaks the next one is spawned and rises out of the ground (`WALL_RISE_TIME` 2.5 s; model attributes `RiseStart`/`RiseTime`, animated by the client `Modules/WallStages`; the gate slab rises on the server) while the army marches on (`Walls.lua`: first wall at `WALL_FWD` 105, last wall = THE GATE at `GATE_FWD` 302 right in front of the outer ring's gate tower, `WALL_SPACING` about 66 studs, 20 high, thickness 3; the gate is 36 high, bronze, thicker). `plotState.wallQueue[layer]` is the wall of that layer. The army fights `plotState.wallLayer`; breaking it crumbles it (debris chunks in the `Debris` collision group), activates the next wall (HP bar billboard moves onto it) and `setFront` moves `plotState.wallFwd` / `wallTargetFwd` / `wallFaceFwd` forward, so the units (which read those every frame) march on to the next wall. The gate is `Economy.isGate(layer)`: when it breaks `PlayerData.setGateCleared` unlocks ascending (not saved) and nothing is left to fight until the player ascends; ascending (`Walls.resetWall`) rebuilds all 4 walls and sends the army back. Static units (towers such as Last Lantern) cannot march: they keep the spot they rose at (`u.staticFwd`) and only their beam `AimPos` follows the front. `GetWallPos` returns a spot in front of the wall being fought. Wall HP/gold per layer and ascension: `Economy.wallHp/wallGold` (tuned with `tools/sim/economy_sim.py`, preset `gate`).
- Collision groups (`Collision` module): `Units` collide with the floor/walls but not with tombstones (`Graves`), each other, players (`Players`), rubble (`Debris`) or thin floor decoration (`Decor`: spawn pad, rune, grass/mud patches; assigned at startup by part name). Any new floor decoration thinner than ~0.5 studs that sits in a lane or graveyard must be added to `FLOOR_DECOR` in `Collision.lua`, otherwise it catches the unit blocks like a wall and units get stuck.
- `Workspace.Shops`: `GraveShopkeeper`, `SellShopkeeper`, `UpgradeShopkeeper`, `ForgeKeeper` - invisible anchor parts with a ProximityPrompt (wired in `ShopNpcs`), sitting on the counters of the stalls in `Workspace.HubDecor`.
- **Forge stall** (`HubDecor.Stall_ForgeKeeper`, built by `tools/studio/build_forge_stall.lua`, safe to re-run, ~315 parts, plaza angle 202.5 near the spawn): a spirit forge on a round dais with a rune ring, an iron anvil with a glowing ingot, a tilted two-layer magic circle above it (models `MagicCircleOuter`/`MagicCircleInner`, tagged `SpinFx` with `SpinSpeed`, `ModelStreamingMode` Atomic; the client module `WorldFx` turns every `SpinFx` model around its PrimaryPart's up axis), a spirit furnace with soul fire and chimney smoke, a ghostly smith, crucibles of the five dusts, a weapon rack and the FORGE lintel. Its prompt fires `OpenForge`.
- **Grave shop stall** (`HubDecor.Stall_GraveShopkeeper`, built by `tools/studio/build_grave_stall.lua`, safe to re-run, ~650 parts, plaza angle 22.5): an undertaker's magic grave shop in a RED theme (crimson glow, ember-orange accents, reddish brick and stone, so it reads differently from the green Forge at a glance): a sarcophagus counter (ledger, coins, candles, a crystal ball in two `SpinFx` rune rings), a skeletal undertaker in a top hat with a lantern staff, display shelves with a miniature of every grave tier's real 3D model (`miniGrave`: a clone of `GraveModels.<key>` scaled to fit 1.3 x 1.9 studs, ground patch hidden, no particles/lights; a simple tombstone if a tier has no model; re-run the stall script after changing a grave model; the minis raise the stall to ~2400 parts), an ornate tombstone floating and turning (`SpinFx`) above an altar in front of a turning summoning halo, an iron fence, coffins, a barrel of shovels, a freshly dug grave with souls rising, floating candles, obelisks with lanterns and an iron arch with the GRAVES sign; a pentagram sigil on the floor where the customer stands. It also places the `Shops.GraveShopkeeper` anchor. `build_coliseum.lua` no longer builds this stall (re-run both stall scripts after it).
- `Workspace.OuterWall`: the fortress ring around the whole arena (inner radius 392, 12 thick, ~70 high; 8 gate towers on the lane axes with a glowing emblem and plot number, 8 round mid-towers with braziers, sconces). Built by `tools/studio/build_ring.lua` (safe to re-run, ~900 parts). **The lane floor in `Walls.lua` runs from `LANE_START_FWD` 24 (edge of the plot Base) to `LANE_END_FWD` 308 and is tied to this radius**: lanes end exactly at the gate towers (plot centre at radius 84 + 308 = 392). If you move the ring update `LANE_END_FWD` and `GATE_FWD` (the last wall must stand just in front of the gate tower).
- **Realm titan (the Gravekeeper)**: `tools/studio/build_titan_gravekeeper.lua` -> `ServerStorage.TitanModels.Gravekeeper` (place file only; from `Images/Entity/...png`; built at 1/12 in design units and scaled; ~175 parts; anchored invisible `HumanoidRootPart` at the hips, bone parts on Motor6Ds Waist/Neck/R+LShoulder/Elbow/Wrist/every finger (`R/LFinger<n>a/b`, thumbs)/Lantern with the pose baked into C0/C1 (the baked pose has the LEFT hand raised = the weave pose); pivot = his feet via `root.PivotOffset` (WorldPivot is ignored with a PrimaryPart); attributes `WallTop`/`WallBack`). Server `Modules/Titans` places one behind every claimed plot's lane (`Titan_<plotIndex>`, sunk in the sea beside the gate tower at `LATERAL` -66, turned 12 degrees to the lane; tag `RealmTitan`; attributes `OwnerId`, `Focus` (the front wall, updated by `Walls.setFront`), `LaneEnd`/`LaneBack`/`LaneLength`); removed when the player leaves. The 8 round mid-towers of the outer ring were removed for his arm (`build_ring.lua` `MID_TOWERS = false`). Client `Modules/TitanAnimator` animates through Motor6D.Transform, slow and heavy (`JOINT_LAG`: torso leads, arms trail; the joints of one arm share a lag): idle = both hands on the battlements (the left arm pose is computed once by `makePoses` from `L_ELBOW_REST`/`L_WRIST_REST`), head follows `Focus`. **Summon**: `Walls.breakWall` sets `SummonAt`/`SummonKind` ("SlamWeave", or "Slam" when the gate fell) unless he is still busy (`titanBusyUntil`, then the wall just rises): he gathers himself (eyes turn red), slams both hands on the wall at `TITAN_IMPACT` 3.1 s (chunks, dust, camera shake for the owner only), a tremor crack runs down the lane (`TREMOR_SPEED` 140) and stuns the army (`plotState.stun`; `Army` stops units the tremor reached and sets `StunFrom`/`StunUntil` on their models; client `Modules/StunFx` draws rune rings sized to the bounding box, `UnitAnimator` freezes them) until the next wall stands; he weaves it with glowing green threads while it rises (`TITAN_WEAVE_RISE` 5.6 s, `TITAN_RISE_TIME` 3.2 s; `spawnWall(..., riseDelay, riseTime)`). When the GATE falls it is his **defeat** (`SummonKind` "Defeat", always played, he stays down: `HOLD_END`): he rears back, reaches over the lane while his eyes go out, lifts his hands off the wall and sinks down and back behind it (the `Sink` pose value: a translation in the Waist Transform, `sinkDir`), the lantern falls into the arena and goes out (`lanternOnGround` sets the Lantern joint's Transform to a world spot). `Walls.resetWall` (ascending) plays "Return" (`TITAN_RETURN` 7.6 s): the raised hands come over the wall first, then the head, the lantern is pulled back up, the hands are lowered slowly onto the battlements. **First visit**: players join on the plaza (HubSpawn, facing their plot; `Plots.initPlotForPlayer`); the titan waits hidden deep behind the wall (`SummonKind` "Waiting", pose `DEEP`, `titanBusyUntil` = huge so breaking walls just rise) until the owner steps onto the plot (server `Titans` Heartbeat check after a 3 s grace period, two checks in a row: right after the join the server can still see the character at its old spawn), then he climbs up ("Enter": the Return climb with the lantern in his hand, `ENTER_TIME` 8.8 s). The giant shovel (`Shovel*` parts) is welded to his anchored root, so it always stands there. Timelines need at least two keys. Known: the resting right hand's fingers still dip slightly into the wall (to fix with the outer wall rework). Timing constants live in both Walls.lua and TitanAnimator.lua: keep them in sync. The model got a detail pass (274 parts: gravestone over the right shoulder, tiered cloak, hood glow + smoke, knuckles, ornate lantern).
- **The portal behind the gate**: server `Modules/Portal`: when the gate falls (`Walls.breakWall`) the lane's gate tower (`OuterWall...GateTower<plotIndex>`) gets attributes `PortalOpen`, `OpenAt`, `OwnerId`, `RealmSize` (`Portal.REALM_SIZE` 5 ascensions per realm), `RealmDone`, `Remaining`, `Ready`; anyone who steps in front of it (12 studs) is thrown back (`RejectAt`, Notice) unless it is the owner and `Ready` (the realm's last ascension, below `MAX_ASCENSIONS` 9): then walking through ascends (`Portal.onPass` = `Plots.ascendPlayer`, which the Ascend button also uses). Closed on ascension (`Walls.resetWall`) and when the player leaves. Client `Modules/PortalFx`: the tower's `GateDoor` bursts outwards and falls flat, a 20 x 30 arched portal (in front of the tower's plinth) with a swirl, five rune stones (lit = done in this realm, the next pulses, all gold when Ready), the text "THE WAY OPENS IN N ASCENSIONS" / "THE WAY IS OPEN", a red flare and shockwave on rejection. Only realm 1 exists: passing the portal is a normal ascension for now.
- **Grave layout and plot look**: `GraveSpot1..10` stand in two rows of 5 along the plot's sides (lateral +-13, forward 14 / 6 / -2 / -10 / -18; `tools/studio/layout_graves.lua`, place file), slots fill closest to the lane first alternating left/right, the tombstones face the free middle path (`Plots.addGraveSlot`). Palette: dark stone (58,60,72)/(88,91,106)/(122,126,142), moss green, glow green (120,255,190), warm lamp (255,190,110).
- `Workspace.HubDecor`: plaza inlays, central monument + beacon, 8 gate paths, rim wall, lamp posts (also on every plot's corners) and the three shop stalls (facing the monument). Workspace attribute `HubSpawn` is where the travel tile's Shops teleports players.

## Lighting

- Lighting is configured in `default.project.json` (Rojo is the source of truth): ShadowMap technology, dusk `ClockTime` 17.7, purple ambient, the `Graveyard Skybox` sky, one `Atmosphere`, `Bloom` and `ColorCorrection`. Edit the JSON, not the Studio properties, or Rojo will overwrite your change. There must be exactly one Sky and one Atmosphere under Lighting (the old place had 12 leftover Skies, now removed).
- Rojo can create duplicate Lighting children when the project file changes while same-named ones already exist in Studio: if you see doubles, delete the extras.
- Terrain water (`tools/studio/set_water.lua`) is stored in the place file only. Inside the outer wall it was replaced by a **lava sea** (`tools/studio/build_lava.lua` -> `Workspace.Lava`: a CrackedLava `Surface` disc 0.9 below the floors, ragged neon pools with rivulets, embers; place file only). Server `Modules/Lava`: touching the `Surface` burns the player for a moment and sends them back to their plot's PlayerSpawn (2 s cooldown, a Notice).
- Neon parts (lamps, runes, beacon, eyes) rely on Bloom (threshold 1.0) to glow; keep new light sources `PointLight.Shadows = false`.
