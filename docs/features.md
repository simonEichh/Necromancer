# Features in detail

Moved out of CLAUDE.md: how each finished feature works (cinematics, in-world panels, Forge, drops, upgrades, UI). Read the section before changing that feature.

## The necromancer's ritual (all pull cinematics)

Client `Modules/NecroRitual` (shared by GraveCinematic, StarSummon, SignatureDescent): the player's own character raises the dead. `NecroRitual.new({hrp, folder, target, smokeAt, feet, color})`; `update(weight, shine, aim)` poses the arms with IKControls (R15 only; avatar joint C0 is read-only), draws wavering ghostly threads (Beams) from the hands to `target`, a violet necromantic circle under the feet and dark smoke, the colour going violet -> rarity colour with `shine`; `look(point, weight)` turns the head/upper body (LookAt IKControl); `absorb(a, unit)` after Continue: a spectral grimoire opens at the left hand, the unit shrinks into it as soul light, the book snaps shut; `restore()` at the end. The character turns to the grave and is anchored during the cinematic; its feet are computed from the character (root minus HipHeight), not from the grave's ground. **Grave cinematic:** after the grave arrives the necromancer works on it (`T_CHANNEL` 1.8 s, a side camera `camChannel`), then it shines in the rarity colour, bursts, absorb after Continue (`ABSORB_TIME`). **Star summon:** starts with the same bait when the grave has a model (`T_BAIT` 3.2 s: the real grave rises and shakes while he casts at it, falls silent at `T_BAIT_SILENCE`), then he lowers his hands and LOOKS UP while the stars light up (no threads to the star); the star camera stands low 11 studs behind him. **Descent:** he only casts during the bait, then looks up while the circles open on their own (divine intervention, the user's wish). All three re-assert `CameraType.Scriptable` every frame (the Studio MCP resets it after an `execute_luau` call, which also makes test screenshots show the default camera: check framing numerically with `WorldToViewportPoint`).

## Outfit items (cosmetics)

Shared `Items.lua` (slots Hand / Head / Back with `SLOT_INFO` (hidden avatar accessory types, scale part), `Defs`, `START`). Models: `ReplicatedStorage.ItemModels.<key>` (place file only), one Accessory each with an invisible `Handle` at the origin holding an Attachment named after the character attachment (RightGripAttachment / HatAttachment / BodyBackAttachment), all other parts welded to it, unanchored, Massless. Built by `tools/studio/build_item_lanternstaff.lua`, `build_item_gravediggerhood.lua`, `build_item_tatteredcape.lua` (the cape's four rows hang on `CapePivot1..3` joined by Motor6Ds `CapeSway1..3`, meant to be swayed by the client; not animated yet). `tools/studio/show_items.lua` puts two R15 dummies wearing them in `Workspace.ItemPreview` (delete before publishing). Data: `profile.items` (owned set) and `profile.equipped[slot]`, saved, sent as `snap.items` (list) / `snap.equipped` -> `State.items` / `State.equipped`; every player owns the start set and new players wear it. Server `Modules/Outfits`: `EquipItem(slot, key | nil)`, `Outfits.apply(player)` clones the equipped items, scales them by the body (`SLOT_INFO.scaleBy`), MOVES every part onto the target attachment before `AddAccessory` (AddAccessory only moves the Handle; WeldConstraints outside the workspace do not pull the rest along) and takes off the avatar's own accessories of the slot's `hides` types (hood: Hat + Hair, cape: Back; put back when the slot is free). Client `Modules/OutfitsPanel` (the "Outfits" tile in the right HUD column under Codex, placeholder Ascend icon, or O; Esc / X / O closes): the camera glides to the front of the anchored character (drag to turn it round), the other ScreenGuis step back, a stone panel on the right with tabs Hand / Head / Back, a card per item with a turning 3D view (`SLOT_INFO.viewTurn` stands hand items upright), details and Equip / Take off. Testing note: simulated keys/clicks from the MCP do not reach the game; open it from a client `execute_luau` (`require(...OutfitsPanel).open()`: a fresh copy whose State is empty, so it shows "Not owned"). Next: cape sway, checking the items in the ritual / Codex poses, a real icon.

## In-world views (WorldView)

Client `Modules/WorldView`: the shared camera part of every panel that takes the player into the 3D world (the user loves these: Codex, Outfits; planned next: the Forge at the anvil, then the Graves rising round the player). `WorldView.open({ keep, openTime, closeTime, fov, goal(s, dt) -> CFrame, blend(s)?, onStep?, onShown?, onClose?, onFinish? })` anchors the character, disables its controls and every other ScreenGui (except `keep`), glides the camera from where it is to `goal` (re-asserting Scriptable every frame) and back, restores everything; `s:close()`; only one view at a time (opening one finishes the old). Keep views upright (no overhead swings), short and skippable.

**The Graves in the world** (client `Modules/GraveScene`, opened by `GravesDock.toggle` (G / the Graves tile); no graves to open or no free spot = the plain panel): the grave tiers you own and can open rise out of the ground in a shallow arc in front of you (staggered, dust), the camera stands behind and beside the character (WorldView), a bar at the bottom (own ScreenGui `GraveSceneUI`: name, count, best odds, < >, Open x1 / xN, All graves, Close), select with the arrows, A/D or a click; a turning MagicCircle marks the selected grave, the character turns to it. The spot search (`findStage`): 24 directions x three arc radii x grave scales 1.5 / 1.15 / 0.9 (smaller when crowded, e.g. the plaza spawn); level floor under every grave, footprints free (measured by the body, not the wide ground patch), open ground between the character and each grave, a free camera spot with clear views of every grave and the arc, and `GraveCinematic.fits(key, g, dir)` (the cinematic's own camera checks, now a shared `stageChecker`; invisible parts, units and rubble never count) for every grave. Open: the others sink, a smaller grave grows to 1.5 in place, `GraveRolling.playRollAnimation` calls `GraveScene.takeStage(graveType)` (ends the view in the same frame) and passes `stage = { G, dir, risen }` to `GraveCinematic.play`, which then starts with the grave already standing (no rise); `DigRejected` -> `GraveScene.cancel()`. `GraveScene.lastFail` records why each layout was refused (testing). Untested with real clicks; door graves (scaled up for tall units) may still fail the check and rise elsewhere.

**The Forge in the world** (client `Modules/ForgeScene`, used by `ForgePanel`): the Forge panel opened AT the stall (`ForgeScene.available()`, the Remote Forge elsewhere keeps the plain panel) opens a WorldView: the camera stands in front of the anvil (`CAM_POS`/`CAM_LOOK` in the stall's pivot frame, shifted right), the player's character is hidden, the stall's two-layer magic circle is taken over from WorldFx (`WorldFx.pause`/`baseOf`) and shrinks (`CIRCLE_REST_SCALE` 0.32) and floats down with the glide (`ForgeScene.setBlend`) to lie flat on the anvil round the unit's feet (during an attempt it rises and spins up, flares on a success, falters and sinks on a failure; it floats back up on close), the panel (own ScreenGui `ForgeUI`) stands on the right (`layoutInWorld`), the selected unit stands small on the ingot (`showUnit`, tagged ProceduralUnit so it idles and shows its level aura). An upgrade (`playUpgrade`): the dust's crucible pours a stream onto the unit (Fortune: a gold coin flies in; Warding: a blue ward ring), the circle rises round it, the furnace flares, the ghostly smith (stall parts `ArmR`/`RaisedHammerHandle`/`RaisedHammerHead`, turned round `SHOULDER` on this client) winds up and WAITS for the server's `UpgradeResult` (`anim.resolve`), strikes beside the unit (flash, sparks, shake), the glow swells the same way for success and failure (the tease, the user wanted it), then success = burst, sparks, "Lv N" rising, `Level` attribute (aura); failure = collapse, smoke, ash (Warding: the ring shatters, the dust flies back). The DataSync is held (`Sync.hold`) until it ends; the result text follows. Skip button (bottom left) and a Quick toggle (`QUICK_SPEED` 2.4). Dismantle: the unit sinks into the anvil and flies into its crucible as dust. The success chance is shown only in the panel (user's call). Untested with real clicks (MCP clicks do not reach the game): the panel buttons, a real upgrade end to end.

## The Codex

Client `Modules/CodexPanel` (the "Codex" tile in the right HUD column under Shop, or C; Esc / X / C closes, A/D or the arrows turn pages): the character holds the grimoire (`NecroRitual.placeBook(open, shown, at)`) up in front of the chest like a book being read (tipped 65 degrees towards them), the camera glides from the normal view to just behind the eyes and looks straight at the pages (always upright: the user got motion sick from an overhead swing), then the book UI (a CanvasGroup, one spread per rarity Common..Primordial, rarity bookmarks) fades in: per unit a 3D portrait (`UnitPortrait`, `viewport.ImageColor3` black = silhouette for undiscovered), name ("???" if undiscovered), "1 in X", base DPS, "Pulled Nx"; "x / y discovered" per rarity and "Codex x / 28". Discovery is server data: `profile.codex[unitType]` = times pulled (saved; `PlayerData.recordPull` in Digging; at load the inventory and the units on graves count as discovered), synced as `snap.codex` -> `State.codex`. The tile uses the Inventory icon as a placeholder. Later idea: rewards for completed pages.

## The Descent (Signature cinematic)

`Modules/SignatureDescent.lua`: the second Signature cinematic. When a Signature is the best pull, `GraveRolling` picks it or the star summon at random (50/50); it needs a grave model (falls back to the star otherwise). About 21 s (`PACE` 0.7 slows the whole timeline; raise it for faster), Skip / Continue. 1. Bait: starts exactly like a hand-opened grave (it rises, shakes) then stops dead. 2. A huge magic circle (radius `TOP_RADIUS` 60, `TOP_HEIGHT` 160 above the floor, raised at the user's request so many circles fit between) flares open, `CIRCLE_COUNT` 14 smaller ones snap open below it faster and faster, all exactly on a cone whose tip is the grave; `LINE_COUNT` 12 light lines draw from the top circle's edge down to the grave. 3. Charging: circles spin against their neighbours faster and faster (moved with `BulkMoveTo`), runes slide down the lines, the world darkens (a ColorCorrection under Lighting, only on this client; keep its tint faint or the gold turns lilac), wind whips dust round the grave. 4. The top circle opens into a starry portal (a glowing deep purple-blue void, so the black figure reads against it); the unit's black silhouette (neon parts gold = glowing eyes, gold rim light) slowly sinks out of it over `T_EMERGE` 2.7 s, fading in, dark wisps trailing (camera OUTSIDE the cone, level with the figure, never below it, slowly zooming onto it as it emerges). 5. It falls down the cone; every circle it passes collapses onto it and becomes a small spinning halo round its body (the user rejected shattering), the camera spirals about 270 degrees round the falling figure, from above it down to a low shot by the grave, with a slight roll. 6. It lands: the grave bursts (GraveOpening, ground fog), all gathered halos flatten and race outwards over the ground as rings of golden runes, gold sparks rain, the world brightens, hero shot. While the rings build, the camera pulls back from the grave to the wide side view so they are seen opening from the side. Colours: a deep gold (255,168,56; the pale SIGNATURE_COLOR blows out to white under bloom) plus the rarity colour. Camera shots: bait, pull-back to the side view during the ring build, wide shot of the whole spire, outside-the-cone zoom onto the emerging figure, spiralling fall, hero. DEV panel: `Descent` button (a Signature unit of the chosen preview rarity). Planned: more Signature cinematics in the same random pool.

## Gacha reveal

`DigGrave(graveType, count)` (server `Digging.lua`) opens up to 10 graves in one request (stops when graves or inventory space run out) and answers with ONE `RollResult` carrying the list of results. Client `GraveRolling` hands the list to `Modules/GachaReveal.play(results, opts)`: an overlay (not full screen) with buildup (the grave shakes, a glow hints at the rarity, big rarities flicker out before the burst), burst (flash, rings, sparks, shake), reveal (a 3D `ViewportFrame` portrait of the unit on a plinth, name, rarity, odds, pity) and Collect. Skip jumps to the reveal. With several results the BEST one gets the dramatic reveal, then a grid of all of them (`Show all`), then `Open xN` for more. **Effects scale smoothly with the rarity rank** (`t = (rank-1)/(maxRank-1)`: buildup length, shake, sparks, rings, fake-out above t 0.55), there is no per-rarity special case, so new rarities work unchanged. Sounds: fill `GachaReveal.Sounds` (buildup, burst, reveal, rare, collect) with sound ids. The portraits need the unit models on the client: `ReplicatedStorage.UnitModels` (a copy of `ServerStorage.Models`, made by `tools/studio/publish_unit_models.lua`; **re-run it after changing or adding a unit model and save the place**). The DEV panel has `Reveal x1` / `Reveal x10` / `Rarity: ...` buttons to preview reveals without rolling. **Graves panel** (`Modules/GravesDock`, the module kept its old name so Sync/ShopPanel/WheelPanel keep calling `refresh()`): a small gold "Graves" stone button on the left (under Inventory) with a badge showing the total number of graves owned; click it or press G to open a stone panel with a card for every grave type (picture, name, count, locked ones show the ascension that unlocks them). Select a card, then `Open x1` or `Open xN` (N = min(10, owned)); the panel closes when an opening starts. Nothing is drawn in the middle of the screen otherwise. `Open all` is planned as a PAID feature: add it as a third button there. Ideas on hold: a 3D camera zoom into the real grave, a full-screen mode.

## Loot drops and the Soul Wisp

A broken wall no longer pays out directly: server `Modules/Drops.lua` (`Drops.spawnWallDrops`, called from `Walls.breakWall`) bursts the wall's gold (3-6 coins) and dust (1-3 crystals per drop entry) into real anchored parts in `Workspace.Drops.Drops_<userId>`, scattered across the lane in front of the broken wall. The server pays a drop when it is collected: the player walks within `MAGNET_RADIUS` 7 of it, or the **Soul Wisp** picks it up, or (more than `MAX_DROPS` 40 on the lane) the oldest is auto-collected at 50 %. The wisp (`profile.collector`, saved) is earned by the first objective, breaking `Drops.COLLECTOR_WALLS` 2 walls (`profile.wallsBroken`, saved; a banner under Place Best shows the progress until then): a server-moved neon ball that flies along the owner's lane (`WISP_SPEED` 14, lingers `WISP_PAUSE` 0.5 s after each pick-up; meant to become a gold upgrade) to the nearest drop, collects it, and rests behind the army. Ascending (`Walls.resetWall` -> `Drops.clearPlot`) pays dust still lying on the lane and drops the gold. **Hit income**: every unit hitting the wall also earns gold ON TOP of the break gold (`Economy.hitIncome(rank, level, ascensions)` = 3 x 1.5^(rank-1) x level multiplier x `INCOME_STEP`s, x coin boost; tuned with the sim preset `income`). `Army.earnHitGold` turns damage into gold at the unit's income / DPS (so hits, arrows, barrages, beams and specials all pay the same gold/s), each unit collects its own, and every `HIT_COIN_INTERVAL` 3.5 s it pops out of the wall where that unit hits as 1-4 coins (`Drops.spawnHitCoins`; stronger units throw more, bigger coins that fly higher, attribute `ArcHeight` read by `Pickups`). The lane holds `MAX_DROPS` 80; the wisp also sweeps every drop within `WISP_SWEEP` 3 of the one it grabs. The HUD's "+X/s" next to Gold is the player attribute `GoldRate` (gold of all coins dropped, walls + hits, over the last 30 s, set by `Drops` once per second). Drops of a player who leaves are lost. The client (`Modules/Pickups.lua`) only animates: the arc out of the wall (attributes `From`, `SpawnTime`), bob and spin, the flight to the player or wisp when the server sets `Collected`, the wisp's trail and particles, and the objective banner. Quest progress (`breakWalls`, `earnGold`) still counts at the wall break. The sim does not model pickups (income is unchanged when everything is collected).

## Realm upgrades (the Upgrades panel)

`Economy.REALM_UPGRADES` (Army Might +8 % damage, Soul Harvest +8 % hit gold, Swift March +10 % marching speed (max 10), Grasping Souls +1.5 studs coin pickup reach (max 8)): bought with gold (`BuyRealmUpgrade`, server `Upgrades`), saved (`profile.realmUpgrades`), kept through the ascensions of a realm and reset with the gold when a new realm starts. Cost `base x growth^level x` the gold steps of the realm's first ascension (`Economy.realmUpgradeCost`), effect `Economy.realmUpgradeEffect`. Applied in `Army.ownerDamageMultiplier` (might; also caches `plotState.marchMult` for the movement), `Army.payHitGold` (harvest) and `Drops` (magnet). To balance them walls get `HP_PER_ASCENSION` +15 % HP per ascension (sim preset `realm`: stage 1 ~12 min, all nine ~40 h). Client `Modules/UpgradesPanel` (replaces the old UpgradePanel): the "Upgrades" tile in the left column (badge = levels affordable now; the Remote Forge tile moved one row down) and the plaza's upgrade stall (`OpenUpgrade`) open it; sections Realm upgrades and Permanent (Luck Boost, Coin Boost). The card icons are placeholders.

## Sell all and locks

The inventory panel's sidebar (`InventoryPanel`) has a SELL ALL section: a rarity button that cycles "up to" Common..Primordial, a "Keep Lv 1+" toggle (on by default), a live preview ("7 units / 345 Gold") and a red Sell button that asks "Sure?" first. Server `Inventory.lua` `SellAll(maxRank, keepUpgraded)` sells every unlocked inventory unit of that rank or lower (1 s rate limit) and answers with a Notice. "Lock mode" turns slot clicks into lock toggles (`ToggleLock`, `PlayerData.toggleLock`); a locked unit shows a gold LOCK tag, is saved with `locked = true` on its inventory entry and can never be sold (single or all) or dismantled. Units on graves are never touched; the lock travels with the unit onto a grave and back (`slot.locked`, `placeInSlot(..., locked)`, saved in `placed`).

## The Forge (unit upgrades and materials)

Units have a `level` (0..`Economy.MAX_UNIT_LEVEL` 10; +30 % damage per level, `Economy.levelMultiplier`) and a `fails` counter (failed upgrades in a row, the pity). They live on the inventory entries (`{ unitType, oneIn, level, fails }`) and on the grave slots (`slot.level`, `slot.fails`) and travel with the unit when it is placed (`PlaceAtGrave`, `Army.placeInSlot(plot, slot, unitType, level, fails)`), recalled, re-deployed by Place Best (which sorts by DPS including the level) and saved (`inventory`, `placed`, `materials` in `Persistence`). Damage of a spawned unit = base x ascension multiplier x level multiplier (`Army.spawnUnit`, `setDamageMultiplier`, `setUnitLevel`); the model gets the attribute `Level` (auras at level milestones are not built yet).

**Materials** are five DUSTS, one per pair of rarities (`Economy.DUSTS` / `DUST_NAMES`, saved under these keys): Stone Dust (Common, Uncommon), Silver Dust (Rare, Cursed), Gold Dust (Legendary, Abyssal), Diamond Dust (Eldritch, Eternal), Star Dust (Primordial; a future rarity above it could share it or get Cosmic Dust). `Economy.dustOf(rarity)` maps a rarity to its dust, `profile.materials[dust]`, `PlayerData.addMaterial/spendMaterial(player, dust, n)`. Old saves with one material per rarity are poured into the matching dust on load. Walls drop dust in `Walls.breakWall` (`Economy.wallMaterials` returns `{dust, amount}`: mostly the dust of rarity ascension + 2, boss walls (every 10th) and the gate add 3x of the dust of the rarity above); dismantling a spare inventory unit gives `Economy.dismantleYield(rank)` of its own dust. **Buy Dust tab** (ForgePanel header tabs Upgrade / Buy Dust; the dust row stays at the bottom): `Economy.DUST_SHOP_STOCK` 10 of each dust per restock, `Economy.dustPrice(d)` = 20 x 50^(d-1) gold (Stone 20, Silver 1K, Gold 50K, Diamond 2.5M, Star 125M), a dust is on sale from ascension `Economy.dustShopUnlock(d)` = 0, 1, 3, 5, 7 (when walls start dropping it). The stock lives in `GraveShop` (refills with the grave rotation every 5 min, sent with `DustStockUpdate(stock, secsLeft)`), purchases go through `BuyDust(dust, qty)` in `UnitUpgrades` (near the Forge, unlocked, in stock, enough gold). Stock is not saved (like the grave shop's). **Catalysts** (the first use for gems; gems are earned from the wheel and quests, Robux gem packs come later): `Economy.CATALYSTS` / `CATALYST_INFO`: Fortune Catalyst (30 Gems, +`FORTUNE_BONUS` 25 % chance on one attempt, capped at 100 %, used up by the attempt) and Warding Catalyst (20 Gems, a failed attempt loses no dust; only used up when it saved the dust). Saved per player (`profile.catalysts`, `PlayerData.spendGems/addCatalyst/useCatalyst`, synced as `snap.catalysts` -> `State.catalysts`). ForgePanel Upgrade tab: a toggle chip per catalyst (owned count; click to use it on the next try, the chance line shows the boost) and a blue gem Buy button; `BuyCatalyst(key, qty)` and `UpgradeUnit(kind, index, useFortune, useWarding)` in UnitUpgrades (buying catalysts works anywhere, upgrading needs the Forge). **Gem Shop** (client `Modules/GemShopPanel`, opened by the Shop icon tile in the right HUD column under Friends, works anywhere; server `Modules/GemShop`): cards for both catalysts, the **Remote Forge** (`Economy.REMOTE_FORGE_PRICE` 250 Gems, one-time, `profile.remoteForge` saved: `ShopNpcs.nearForge` returns true for its owners, a Forge tile appears in the left HUD column under Graves and the Forge panel no longer closes when walking away) and **Soul Wisp Speed** (`profile.wispLevel` 0..`WISP_MAX_LEVEL` 5, saved; `Economy.wispLevelPrice(L)` = 40 x 2^L, `wispSpeed(L)` = 14 x (1 + 0.3L), `wispPause(L)`, read by `Drops`; needs the wisp). `BuyGemItem("RemoteForge" | "WispSpeed")`. The Shop and Forge tiles use the Gems and Gold Dust icons until real ones are painted. Planned: a "Get Gems" section with Robux packs.

**Level auras** (client `Modules/LevelAuras.lua`, from the model attribute `Level`; every tier keeps the layers below and adds one, necromantic look, same for every unit): level 3 souls rising from a ring around the feet (NO visible ground circle, the user rejected it), 6 + three soul orbs with trails orbiting the body and a soft light, 9 + dark green necrotic flames (kept dim: LightEmission ~0.35) and black miasma, 10 + violet flame core, a crown of five small skulls circling above the head and a flash when reached. Sizes follow the unit's bounding box; auras exist only within 140 studs of the camera; no Highlights (31 per client limit). The DEV panel's `Lv` button spawns dev units at level 0/3/6/9/10 to preview them. The grave label shows `Unit  Lv N`; the HUD shows a dust row above Gold/Gems.

**Rules** (all in `Economy`, tuned with the sim): going from level L to L+1 costs `unitUpgradeCost(L, rarity)` = ceil(4 x 1.55^L) of the unit's dust (x1.5 for the higher rarity of a pair) and succeeds with `upgradeChance(L, fails)` = max(0.2, 1 - 0.08L) + 0.10 x fails (max 1). A failure costs half the materials, never lowers the level and raises the next chance. Server `Modules/UnitUpgrades.lua` handles `UpgradeUnit(kind "inv"|"grave", index)` and `DismantleUnit(invSlot)` (small rate limit), answers with `UpgradeResult(success, unitType, newLevel, chance)` and the usual DataSync (which now carries `materials` and `placed`; `PlayerData.placedProvider` is set by Army). Client `Modules/ForgePanel.lua` (opens ONLY at the Forge stall via `OpenForge`, closes when the player walks more than 22 studs away; there is no side button or F key any more, a premium "Remote Forge" may unlock that later; the server refuses UpgradeUnit/DismantleUnit unless `ShopNpcs.nearForge(player)`, 25 studs): list of units on graves + inventory, details, cost, chance, Upgrade/Dismantle, a row with all nine materials.

## Star summon (cinematic, prototype)

`Modules/StarSummon.lua`: `StarSummon.play(results, { onDone, speed })` is a cinematic rendered in the 3D world for a pull (client only, scripted camera, controls and the main ScreenGui switched off while it runs, restored at the end; a Skip button ends it). One star per result hangs in the sky above a grave in front of the player (tinted by rarity, flare drawn on a BillboardGui); the BEST one builds up an aura (pulsing rings), flares, then a cut to a side chase camera follows it falling with fire/smoke, it crashes on the grave (shock rings, dust, embers, debris in the `Debris` collision group, crater with a glowing rim, white flash, camera shake) and the unit's model (`ReplicatedStorage.UnitModels`, tagged `ProceduralUnit`, using the `RiseStart`/`RiseTime` rise animation) climbs out of the crater with its name and rarity. Everything scales with the rarity rank `t` (aura, fall time, impact). The DEV panel has `Star x1` / `Star x10` buttons (uses the chosen preview rarity). **Hooked into real pulls**: `GraveRolling.playRollAnimation` plays it when the best result has rank >= `STAR_MIN_RANK` (5 = Legendary), then shows the overlay with `skipShow = true` (straight to the result: Collect, odds, the results grid); lower pulls keep the short overlay show; if the cinematic errors the overlay plays normally. The hero shot frames the whole unit (its `Height` attribute x scale, floating units included: the camera backs off and looks at the middle of the body). Once the unit is up the cinematic waits: a Continue stone button (or Space / Enter / E / gamepad A) ends it while the camera keeps orbiting; the local character is hidden (`LocalTransparencyModifier`) during the cinematic so it never blocks the camera. The stage is found by `StarSummon.play`: a spot about 30 studs from the player in the first direction (look direction first, then around) whose camera paths are free of walls/stalls (raycasts), so it works anywhere in the arena. No sounds yet.

## UI roadmap (planned, not started)

Dark gothic restyle, no emojis anywhere, 3D rendered unit portraits (ViewportFrame) for the inventory and a new gacha reveal, built on one `Theme` module + small UI kit, restyled panel by panel (HUD/hotbar first). **All emojis have been removed from the UI** (the `Units.Emoji` / `GameData` emoji tables and the wheel/upgrade icon lists are now empty strings; the icon spots on the HUD side buttons, grave dock, wheel cards and upgrade rows are empty until real icons exist (the hotbar and inventory slots show 3D unit portraits via `Modules/UnitPortrait`, which needs `ReplicatedStorage.UnitModels`); currencies are written as words: Gold, Gems). **Painted icons** (made with ChatGPT from a shared style prompt: chunky hand-painted gothic, transparent PNG, no text): sources in `Images/UI`, cropped/resized to 512 px in `design/icons`, uploaded with the Studio MCP `upload_image` (serve the folder over http first), ids in `Theme.Image.Icon` (Ascend, Inventory, Graves, Quests, Friends, Gold, Gems, Wheel, Luck, GoldBoost) and `Theme.Image.Dust` (one per dust: HUD dust bar, Forge material chips). The spin wheel button is an icon tile (label = spins / countdown). **Panel frame**: `Theme.Image.PanelFrame` (painted stone border with bronze skull corners, middle made transparent) is 9-sliced round every `Theme.panel` (`PANEL_SLICE`, `PANEL_SLICE_SCALE` 0.34, hangs `PANEL_FRAME_MARGIN` 28 px outside the panel so layouts are unchanged; ZIndex panel - 1, BEHIND the panel, so it never covers content near the edges). ## Batch dismantle and the upgrade refund

The Forge panel's third tab **Dismantle** (`ForgePanel`, `salvage` frame): "Up to" rarity cycle, "Keep Lv 1+" toggle (on by default), a live preview (units, dust per type with icons) and a list of exactly which units it takes (grouped "Skelly  x3", rarity stripe, Lv tag, highest rarity first), red "Dismantle all" with a "Sure?" confirm. Server `UnitUpgrades` `DismantleAll(maxRank, keepUpgraded)` (Forge only like single dismantle: `ShopNpcs.nearForge`, Remote Forge counts; locked units and units on graves never touched; 1 s rate limit) answers `DismantleAllResult(count, totals)` BEFORE the deferred DataSync, so the panel holds the sync (`Sync.hold`) while `ForgeScene.playBatchDismantle` plays one combined burst (anvil flash and fire flare, one dust stream per dust type into its crucible, ~2 s, Skip/Quick). **Refund:** `Economy.dismantleDust(rarity, level)` = the base `dismantleYield(rank)` + `DISMANTLE_REFUND` (50 %) of the dust the unit's levels cost (successful steps only); single and batch dismantle both use it (the user: low-rarity units fall off fast and upgrades should not feel wasted).
