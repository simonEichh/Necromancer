## The necromancer's ritual (all pull cinematics)

Client `Modules/NecroRitual` (shared by GraveCinematic, StarSummon, SignatureDescent): the player's own character raises the dead. `NecroRitual.new({hrp, folder, target, smokeAt, feet, color})`; `update(weight, shine, aim)` poses the arms with IKControls (R15 only; avatar joint C0 is read-only), draws wavering ghostly threads (Beams) from the hands to `target`, a violet necromantic circle under the feet and dark smoke, the colour going violet -> rarity colour with `shine`; `look(point, weight)` turns the head/upper body (LookAt IKControl); `absorb(a, unit)` after Continue: a spectral grimoire opens at the left hand, the unit shrinks into it as soul light, the book snaps shut; `restore()` at the end. The character turns to the grave and is anchored during the cinematic; its feet are computed from the character (root minus HipHeight), not from the grave's ground. **Grave cinematic:** after the grave arrives the necromancer works on it (`T_CHANNEL` 1.8 s, a side camera `camChannel`), then it shines in the rarity colour, bursts, absorb after Continue (`ABSORB_TIME`). **Star summon:** starts with the same bait when the grave has a model (`T_BAIT` 3.2 s: the real grave rises and shakes while he casts at it, falls silent at `T_BAIT_SILENCE`), then he lowers his hands and LOOKS UP while the stars light up (no threads to the star); the star camera stands low 11 studs behind him. **Descent:** he only casts during the bait, then looks up while the circles open on their own (divine intervention, the user's wish). All three re-assert `CameraType.Scriptable` every frame (the Studio MCP resets it after an `execute_luau` call, which also makes test screenshots show the default camera: check framing numerically with `WorldToViewportPoint`).

## In-world views (WorldView)

**The Lucky Wheel in the plaza:** `Workspace.LuckyWheel` is a physical seven-section bone-and-iron wheel, built by `tools/studio/build_wheel.lua`. The existing HUD wheel tile and the wheel's E prompt open the same upright WorldView (`Modules/WheelScene`), with a dark stone controls panel on the right (`Modules/WheelPanel`, own ScreenGui `WheelUI`); the character stays in place and is hidden locally until the view closes. Spin turns the actual rotor six full revolutions then decelerates over 5 s to the server's reward at the fixed skull pointer; every client sees the shared spin from the replicated `SpinAt`, `SpinFrom`, `SpinTo`, `SpinDuration` and `SpinWinner` attributes. One shared spin at a time; rejected requests consume no spin, release held sync, and show a reason. Closing the view during a spin restores the camera/character and still completes the reward reveal. Desktop and touch use the gold Spin button.

**Rewards reworked (approved and implemented 2026-10-16):** shared `WheelRewards` owns odds, order, labels and amounts;
`WheelPrizes.resolve` is used by the server award and the client's progression-aware preview. Rewards resolve when
the spin is accepted; the reveal uses that exact prize, even if progression changes during the animation.

**Studio DEV charges:** `+10 Wheel Spins` next to Unlock Codex in the DEV panel fires `DevGiveWheelSpins`.
`DevTools` handles it only in Studio, adds a fixed ten to the existing server wheel balance, marks the profile dirty
for normal saving, and immediately sends `WheelSpinsUpdate` to refresh the HUD and wheel view. It leaves the
15-minute free-spin timer intact; client-supplied amounts are ignored. The panel is absent outside Studio and the
server registers no dev handler there.
Verified in Studio: two requests added 10 charges each (including a request with an extra client amount), the wheel
count updated immediately without resetting its timer, and a successful spin consumed one charge and awarded dust.
No game-script errors during the check.

## Gacha reveal

`DigGrave(graveType, count)` (server `Digging.lua`) opens up to 10 graves in one request (stops when graves or inventory space run out) and answers with ONE `RollResult` carrying the list of results. Client `GraveRolling` hands the list to `Modules/GachaReveal.play(results, opts)`: an overlay (not full screen) with buildup (the grave shakes, a glow hints at the rarity, big rarities flicker out before the burst), burst (flash, rings, sparks, shake), reveal (a 3D `ViewportFrame` portrait of the unit on a plinth, name, rarity, odds, pity) and Collect. Skip jumps to the reveal. With several results the BEST one gets the dramatic reveal, then a grid of all of them (`Show all`), then `Open xN` for more. **Effects scale smoothly with the rarity rank** (`t = (rank-1)/(maxRank-1)`: buildup length, shake, sparks, rings, fake-out above t 0.55), there is no per-rarity special case, so new rarities work unchanged. Sounds: fill `GachaReveal.Sounds` (buildup, burst, reveal, rare, collect) with sound ids. The portraits need the unit models on the client: `ReplicatedStorage.UnitModels` (a copy of `ServerStorage.Models`, made by `tools/studio/publish_unit_models.lua`; **re-run it after changing or adding a unit model and save the place**). The DEV panel has `Reveal x1` / `Reveal x10` / `Rarity: ...` buttons to preview reveals without rolling. **Graves panel** (`Modules/GravesDock`, the module kept its old name so Sync/ShopPanel/WheelPanel keep calling `refresh()`): a small gold "Graves" stone button on the left (under Inventory) with a badge showing the total number of graves owned; click it or press G to open a stone panel with a card for every grave type (picture, name, count, locked ones show the ascension that unlocks them). Select a card, then `Open x1` or `Open xN` (N = min(10, owned)); the panel closes when an opening starts. Nothing is drawn in the middle of the screen otherwise. `Open all` is planned as a PAID feature: add it as a third button there. Ideas on hold: a 3D camera zoom into the real grave, a full-screen mode.

## Loot drops and the Soul Wisp

A broken wall no longer pays out directly: server `Modules/Drops.lua` (`Drops.spawnWallDrops`, called from `Walls.breakWall`) bursts the wall's gold (3-6 coins) and dust (1-3 crystals per drop entry) into real anchored parts in `Workspace.Drops.Drops_<userId>`, scattered across the lane in front of the broken wall. The server pays a drop when it is collected: the player walks within `MAGNET_RADIUS` 7 of it, or the **Soul Wisp** picks it up, or (more than `MAX_DROPS` 40 on the lane) the oldest is auto-collected at 50 %. The wisp (`profile.collector`, saved) is earned by the first objective, breaking `Drops.COLLECTOR_WALLS` 2 walls (`profile.wallsBroken`, saved; a banner under Place Best shows the progress until then): a server-moved neon ball that flies along the owner's lane (`WISP_SPEED` 14, lingers `WISP_PAUSE` 0.5 s after each pick-up; meant to become a gold upgrade) to the nearest drop, collects it, and rests behind the army. Ascending (`Walls.resetWall` -> `Drops.clearPlot`) pays dust still lying on the lane and drops the gold. **Hit income**: every unit hitting the wall also earns gold ON TOP of the break gold (`Economy.hitIncome(rank, level, ascensions)` = 3 x 1.5^(rank-1) x level multiplier x `INCOME_STEP`s, x coin boost; tuned with the sim preset `income`). `Army.earnHitGold` turns damage into gold at the unit's income / DPS (so hits, arrows, barrages, beams and specials all pay the same gold/s), each unit collects its own, and every `HIT_COIN_INTERVAL` 3.5 s it pops out of the wall where that unit hits as 1-4 coins (`Drops.spawnHitCoins`; stronger units throw more, bigger coins that fly higher, attribute `ArcHeight` read by `Pickups`). The lane holds `MAX_DROPS` 80; the wisp also sweeps every drop within `WISP_SWEEP` 3 of the one it grabs. The HUD's "+X/s" next to Gold is the player attribute `GoldRate` (gold of all coins dropped, walls + hits, over the last 30 s, set by `Drops` once per second). Drops of a player who leaves are lost. The client (`Modules/Pickups.lua`) only animates: the arc out of the wall (attributes `From`, `SpawnTime`), bob and spin, the flight to the player or wisp when the server sets `Collected`, the wisp's trail and particles, and the objective banner. Quest progress (`breakWalls`, `earnGold`) still counts at the wall break. The sim does not model pickups (income is unchanged when everything is collected).

## Sell all and locks

The inventory panel's sidebar (`InventoryPanel`) has a SELL ALL section: a rarity button that cycles "up to" Common..Primordial, a "Keep Lv 1+" toggle (on by default), a live preview ("7 units / 345 Gold") and a red Sell button that asks "Sure?" first. Server `Inventory.lua` `SellAll(maxRank, keepUpgraded)` sells every unlocked inventory unit of that rank or lower (1 s rate limit) and answers with a Notice. "Lock mode" turns slot clicks into lock toggles (`ToggleLock`, `PlayerData.toggleLock`); a locked unit shows a gold LOCK tag, is saved with `locked = true` on its inventory entry and can never be sold (single or all) or dismantled. Units on graves are never touched; the lock travels with the unit onto a grave and back (`slot.locked`, `placeInSlot(..., locked)`, saved in `placed`).

Units have a `level` (0..`Economy.MAX_UNIT_LEVEL` 10; +30 % damage per level, `Economy.levelMultiplier`) and a `fails` counter (failed upgrades in a row, the pity). They live on the inventory entries (`{ unitType, oneIn, level, fails }`) and on the grave slots (`slot.level`, `slot.fails`) and travel with the unit when it is placed (`PlaceAtGrave`, `Army.placeInSlot(plot, slot, unitType, level, fails)`), recalled, re-deployed by Place Best (which sorts by DPS including the level) and saved (`inventory`, `placed`, `materials` in `Persistence`). Damage of a spawned unit = base x ascension multiplier x level multiplier (`Army.spawnUnit`, `setDamageMultiplier`, `setUnitLevel`); the model gets the attribute `Level` (auras at level milestones are not built yet).

**Materials** are five DUSTS, one per pair of rarities (`Economy.DUSTS` / `DUST_NAMES`, saved under these keys): Stone Dust (Common, Uncommon), Silver Dust (Rare, Cursed), Gold Dust (Legendary, Abyssal), Diamond Dust (Eldritch, Eternal), Star Dust (Primordial; a future rarity above it could share it or get Cosmic Dust). `Economy.dustOf(rarity)` maps a rarity to its dust, `profile.materials[dust]`, `PlayerData.addMaterial/spendMaterial(player, dust, n)`. Old saves with one material per rarity are poured into the matching dust on load. Walls drop dust in `Walls.breakWall` (`Economy.wallMaterials` returns `{dust, amount}`: mostly the dust of rarity ascension + 2, boss walls (every 10th) and the gate add 3x of the dust of the rarity above); dismantling a spare inventory unit gives `Economy.dismantleYield(rank)` of its own dust. **Buy Dust tab** (ForgePanel header tabs Upgrade / Buy Dust; the dust row stays at the bottom): `Economy.DUST_SHOP_STOCK` 10 of each dust per restock, `Economy.dustPrice(d)` = 20 x 50^(d-1) gold (Stone 20, Silver 1K, Gold 50K, Diamond 2.5M, Star 125M), a dust is on sale from ascension `Economy.dustShopUnlock(d)` = 0, 1, 3, 5, 7 (when walls start dropping it). The stock lives in `GraveShop` (refills with the grave rotation every 5 min, sent with `DustStockUpdate(stock, secsLeft)`), purchases go through `BuyDust(dust, qty)` in `UnitUpgrades` (near the Forge, unlocked, in stock, enough gold). Stock is not saved (like the grave shop's). **Catalysts** (the first use for gems; gems are earned from the wheel and quests, Robux gem packs come later): `Economy.CATALYSTS` / `CATALYST_INFO`: Fortune Catalyst (30 Gems, +`FORTUNE_BONUS` 25 % chance on one attempt, capped at 100 %, used up by the attempt) and Warding Catalyst (20 Gems, a failed attempt loses no dust; only used up when it saved the dust). Saved per player (`profile.catalysts`, `PlayerData.spendGems/addCatalyst/useCatalyst`, synced as `snap.catalysts` -> `State.catalysts`). ForgePanel Upgrade tab: a toggle chip per catalyst (owned count; click to use it on the next try, the chance line shows the boost) and a blue gem Buy button; `BuyCatalyst(key, qty)` and `UpgradeUnit(kind, index, useFortune, useWarding)` in UnitUpgrades (buying catalysts works anywhere, upgrading needs the Forge). **Gem Shop** (client `Modules/GemShopPanel`, opened by the Shop icon tile in the right HUD column under Friends, works anywhere; server `Modules/GemShop`): cards for both catalysts, the **Remote Forge** (`Economy.REMOTE_FORGE_PRICE` 250 Gems, one-time, `profile.remoteForge` saved: `ShopNpcs.nearForge` returns true for its owners, a Forge tile appears in the sixth left HUD slot, under Army and the Forge panel no longer closes when walking away) and **Soul Wisp Speed** (`profile.wispLevel` 0..`WISP_MAX_LEVEL` 5, saved; `Economy.wispLevelPrice(L)` = 40 x 2^L, `wispSpeed(L)` = 14 x (1 + 0.3L), `wispPause(L)`, read by `Drops`; needs the wisp). `BuyGemItem("RemoteForge" | "WispSpeed")`. The Shop and Forge tiles use the Gems and Gold Dust icons until real ones are painted. Planned: a "Get Gems" section with Robux packs.

**Level auras** (client `Modules/LevelAuras.lua`, from the model attribute `Level`; every tier keeps the layers below and adds one, necromantic look, same for every unit): level 3 souls rising from a ring around the feet (NO visible ground circle, the user rejected it), 6 + three soul orbs with trails orbiting the body and a soft light, 9 + dark green necrotic flames (kept dim: LightEmission ~0.35) and black miasma, 10 + violet flame core, a crown of five small skulls circling above the head and a flash when reached. Sizes follow the unit's bounding box; auras exist only within 140 studs of the camera; no Highlights (31 per client limit). The DEV panel's `Lv` button spawns dev units at level 0/3/6/9/10 to preview them. The grave label shows `Unit  Lv N`; the HUD shows a dust row above Gold/Gems.

**Rules** (all in `Economy`, tuned with the sim): going from level L to L+1 costs `unitUpgradeCost(L, rarity)` = ceil(4 x 1.55^L) of the unit's dust (x1.5 for the higher rarity of a pair) and succeeds with `upgradeChance(L, fails)` = max(0.2, 1 - 0.08L) + 0.10 x fails (max 1). A failure costs half the materials, never lowers the level and raises the next chance. Server `Modules/UnitUpgrades.lua` handles `UpgradeUnit(kind "inv"|"grave", index)` and `DismantleUnit(invSlot)` (small rate limit), answers with `UpgradeResult(success, unitType, newLevel, chance)` and the usual DataSync (which now carries `materials` and `placed`; `PlayerData.placedProvider` is set by Army). ## Star summon (cinematic, prototype)

`Modules/StarSummon.lua`: `StarSummon.play(results, { onDone, speed })` is a cinematic rendered in the 3D world for a pull (client only, scripted camera, controls and the main ScreenGui switched off while it runs, restored at the end; a Skip button ends it). One star per result hangs in the sky above a grave in front of the player (tinted by rarity, flare drawn on a BillboardGui); the BEST one builds up an aura (pulsing rings), flares, then a cut to a side chase camera follows it falling with fire/smoke, it crashes on the grave (shock rings, dust, embers, debris in the `Debris` collision group, crater with a glowing rim, white flash, camera shake) and the unit's model (`ReplicatedStorage.UnitModels`, tagged `ProceduralUnit`, using the `RiseStart`/`RiseTime` rise animation) climbs out of the crater with its name and rarity. Everything scales with the rarity rank `t` (aura, fall time, impact). The DEV panel has `Star x1` / `Star x10` buttons (uses the chosen preview rarity). **Hooked into real pulls**: `GraveRolling.playRollAnimation` plays it when the best result has rank >= `STAR_MIN_RANK` (5 = Legendary), then shows the overlay with `skipShow = true` (straight to the result: Collect, odds, the results grid); lower pulls keep the short overlay show; if the cinematic errors the overlay plays normally. The hero shot frames the whole unit (its `Height` attribute x scale, floating units included: the camera backs off and looks at the middle of the body). Once the unit is up the cinematic waits: a Continue stone button (or Space / Enter / E / gamepad A) ends it while the camera keeps orbiting; the local character is hidden (`LocalTransparencyModifier`) during the cinematic so it never blocks the camera. The stage is found by `StarSummon.play`: a spot about 30 studs from the player in the first direction (look direction first, then around) whose camera paths are free of walls/stalls (raycasts), so it works anywhere in the arena. No sounds yet.
